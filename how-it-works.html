Hero Tips

The heroes that grow the fastest are usually the ones being honest and completing the most real-life missions. Every mission gives XP, and how players choose to upgrade their stats can completely change how their hero performs in battle.

How Battles Work

Battles are fast, strategic, and easy to learn. Every hero has different strengths, and how players upgrade their stats can completely change the outcome of a battle. (Click the images below for more details on each battle tip!)

1. Speed Goes First

The hero with the highest speed starts first during battle, then it alternates every other player. If speed is a tie, the youngest hero starts first.

2. Use Stamina Carefully

Attacking and defending use stamina. Players must decide when to attack and when to rest.

3. Attack vs. Defense

Strong attacks can break through defenses, but defensive heroes can survive longer battles.

4. Focus or Self-Heal

Sometimes the best move is not attacking. Smart players know when to heal, recover stamina, or prepare for the next turn.

5. Strategy Beats Randomness

Balanced teams and smart decisions often win against stronger heroes or bosses.

How It Works

Start with a hero, complete missions, earn XP, and level up through real-world challenges.

Every player begins with a free starter hero and their first real-life missions. To continue leveling up and expanding their adventure, players can collect additional mission books and stronger heroes from the shop. Different heroes, stats, and future abilities allow players to build unique strategies and battle styles over time.

Expand Your Adventure

Every Battle Feels Different

Different heroes, stats, and decisions can completely change the outcome of a battle. Some players build fast heroes that start first and control the order of battle, while others focus on defense, stamina, or long-term strategy. As new heroes and mission books are collected, players can experiment with different combinations and playstyles.

Growth That Continues Over Time

The more missions players complete in real life, the more strategies they unlock for battling. Stronger heroes, better stat builds, and smarter strategies all come from consistent progress over time. Small daily actions slowly turn into stronger characters, better decisions, and more exciting battles.
