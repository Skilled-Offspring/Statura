How Battles Work

Battles are fast, strategic, and easy to learn. Every hero has different strengths, and how players upgrade their stats can completely change the outcome of a battle.

Speed Goes First

The hero with the highest speed starts first during battle, then it alternates every other player. If speed is a tie, the youngest hero starts first.

Use Stamina Carefully

Attacking and defending use stamina. Players must decide when to attack and when to rest.

Attack vs. Defense

Strong attacks can break through defenses, but defensive heroes can survive longer battles.

Focus or Self-Heal

Sometimes the best move is not attacking. Smart players know when to heal, recover stamina, or prepare for the next turn.

Strategy Beats Randomness

Balanced teams and smart decisions often win against stronger heroes or bosses.
